Questions

What do I need to send?

PDF plans: floor plan, elevations and site plan. Landscape and roof plans add detail. Your builder, architect or council can supply them.

How accurate is it?

It follows the dimensions on your plans. The plans are read twice and the two reads are checked against each other. Anything not drawn is left off.

What if I don't like the preview?

Don't order. Or ask for one free round of changes.

What is the Christmas tree version?

Your house about 60 mm across, with a ribbon through the roof. A$60. You see it in 3D before you order.

How big is it?

1:100, so 10 mm per metre. A typical house on a 32 m block is about 320 mm long. Blocks that don't fit the 340 x 320 mm print bed are cropped around the house. Large houses are done at 1:150. The preview shows the final size.

When do I pay?

When you order, your card is held. I confirm within 24 hours and only then is it charged. If I don't confirm, the hold is released.

What happens to my files?

Your model is kept so you can reorder or download it later. Your name and email are deleted 12 months after the order is finished. Print files are for personal use only. Your PDF plans are not stored.

Can I give one as a gift?

Yes, if you can get the plans.

Can I use my plans?

Plans usually belong to whoever drew them. Check with them before you upload.

Pickup or post?

Pickup in Ferny Hills is free. Post is Australia Post at cost, shown before you pay.
