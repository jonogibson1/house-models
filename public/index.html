Before you upload

What do I need to send?

The PDF plans from your builder, architect or designer: the floor plan, the elevations and the site plan. A landscape plan and a roof plan add more detail. If you can't find them, your council can usually supply a copy for a fee.

How accurate is it?

It is built from your plans and nothing else, as one white piece, like an architect's presentation model. The walls and roof come from the dimensions; every window and door is set into the walls with a frame; weatherboards and board cladding show as lines, render panels stand slightly proud of brickwork; open alfresco and porch areas stand on their posts. On the lot you get what the site and landscape plans show: driveway, paths, slabs, garden beds, decks, pools, fences, retaining walls and fixtures like the air conditioner and letterbox. No trees, no lawn texture, nothing invented. Your plans are read once and then checked a second time against the drawings, item by item.

What if I don't like the preview?

Then it costs you nothing. Ask for your one free set of changes, or leave it there.

What is the Christmas tree version?

The same model of your house, printed small enough to hang on the tree, about 65 mm across for a typical home. A ribbon runs down through the roof and walls and is knotted underneath. It is A$60, and you see it in 3D before you order, the same as the shelf model.

How big is it?

1:100, so every metre is 10 mm of model. A typical house on a 32 metre lot comes out about 320 mm long, sitting on a base of its lot with the landscaping. Lots too big for the printer's 340 x 320 mm plate are cropped around the house, and homes too big for the plate are made at 1:150. The preview shows exactly what you get.

When do I pay?

After you have seen your preview and decided to order. Your card is held at that point, I review the order within 24 hours, and it is charged only when I confirm I can make it. If I can't, or I don't confirm in time, the hold is released.

What happens to the print files?

Your model is kept with your order, so it can be printed again or downloaded again later from your order link. If you buy the print files only, you download them from your order link once I confirm, as one STL file with the printer settings. The files are for your own personal use: please don't sell them or share them publicly, because they are made from plans someone else drew. Your PDF plans are never stored here.

Can I give one as a gift?

Yes, as long as you can get the plans. It suits a housewarming, a handover day or the anniversary of moving in.

Is it OK to use my plans?

Plans usually belong to the designer or builder who drew them, so check with them before you upload.

Pickup or post?

Pickup from Ferny Hills is free. Postage is Australia Post Parcel Post at cost, worked out from the boxed size and weight of your model and shown before you pay.
